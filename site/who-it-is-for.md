# Who Alcove is for

Alcove is for two organizations that each hold records about people and need to know which people they share, without handing each other their files.

## Organizations

- Alcove is built for record linkage between two partner agencies that have a signed data sharing agreement.
  It is designed to work within the policy and infrastructure constraints typical of government agencies.
- It grew out of a community of practice between California homeless Continuums of Care and Managed Care Organizations spanning twelve counties ([About](about.md)).
- It is designed for personal information.
  Health records and education records can be suitable under the conditions the [compliance guide](../docs/COMPLIANCE.md#intended-use-and-data-classification) sets out.
  Alcove has not been assessed for criminal justice information or federal tax information.

## People in those organizations

- Analysts with a spreadsheet and a browser, running a first or occasional exchange.
- Partners who received an invitation to an exchange.
- IT staff fitting recurring exchanges into a data pipeline.
- Program officers evaluating the software.
- Security, privacy, and compliance reviewers.
- Developers contributing to the project.

The [reading guide for each role](../docs/README.md#role-based-reading-guide) shows where each of them starts.
To run an exchange, [choose a guide](../docs/guides/index.md).

## What stays with your organization

- Your organization runs the software and is the only controller of the data it processes.
- The decision to disclose, its legal basis, the data sharing agreement, and the risk assessment stay with your organization.
  Alcove runs the exchange the two parties set up and does not decide whether a disclosure is lawful.

An agency security review starts with [Shared responsibility](../docs/SHARED_RESPONSIBILITY.md).
