# Contact

Alcove is maintained by a small team at Georgetown University's Massive Data Institute, on a best-effort basis.

## Questions and bug reports

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/georgetown-mdi/alcove/issues).
Tag a question with the `question` label, and put "evaluation" in the title of a question about evaluating Alcove for your agency.
[Getting support](../SUPPORT.md) lists what to include in a bug report.
There is no discussion forum or mailing list.

## Security reports

Do not report a vulnerability in a public issue.
Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, as the [security policy](../SECURITY.md) describes.
The policy also gives an email address for when you cannot use GitHub.

## Working together

To talk about collaborating on Alcove, [email Eric Giannella](mailto:eric.giannella@georgetown.edu), who leads its pilot deployments.

## Contributing

The [contributing guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) covers building and testing Alcove, the conventions a change follows, and how pull requests are reviewed.

## What to expect

There is no commercial support, paid engagement, or service-level agreement.
Bug reports and security reports come before feature requests and evaluation questions, and the response times in the security policy apply only to security reports.
