- - -> In delete mode, a frame the sender has marked as one the peer will not read (`markLastSentUnread`) is not drained, and `cleanup()` deletes it at once.
- - -> The key exchange marks one frame so: a responder's answer to an abort holding a behind notice that verifies, whose initiator fails as it sends that abort and reads no reply ([PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md#p-256-authenticated-key-exchange), "When no secret matches").
- - -> A stale responder's run therefore ends at its refusal rather than after the drain bound.
