- - -> `single-pass-over-limit` | `limit` (one of `browser-dataset`, `value-slots`, `frame`, `distinct-values`), and the facts of that limit: for `browser-dataset`, `recordCount` and `linkageKeyCount` (each a non-negative integer) and `fansOut` (optional; `true` only); for `value-slots`, `local` and `partner` (each an object of `effectiveKeyCount` and `recordCount`, non-negative integers), `limitSlots` (non-negative integer), and `localFansOut` and `partnerFansOut` (each optional; `true` only); for `frame`, `frame` (one of `reply`, `request`) and `frameBytes` and `limitBytes` (each a non-negative integer); for `distinct-values`, `limitValues` (non-negative integer) | A single-pass exchange's data was over a limit: a browser party's declared records across the linkage keys, before contact (`browser-dataset`); either party's declared value slots in an exchange with a browser, at the terms exchange (`value-slots`, [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md#the-single-pass-dataset-ceiling-receiver-memory-and-masking-compute)); a single-pass frame over the bytes one encrypted message holds on a file-sync connection (`frame`); or this party's distinct linkage key values over the most one single-pass set holds (`distinct-values`). A fan-out fact states that a step splitting values or expanding a key raises that party's count. A `value-slots` cause in which neither party is over `limitSlots` is refused.
