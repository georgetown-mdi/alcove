- - -> **The control frame limit.**
- - -> The relay reads at most 60 control frames from one socket between two of its pings (`RELAY_MAX_CONTROL_FRAMES`), counting each ping and each pong that answers none of the relay's pings, from the socket's upgrade on.
- - -> A socket past it is closed with 4011, and dropped if it sends another while the relay waits on its close.
- - -> A pong answering one of the relay's pings is not counted, so a client answering a backlog of pings at once stays within the limit.
- - -> The clients send no ping and answer each of the relay's pings: Node v26.13's `WebSocket`, the command-line client's, sent one pong for each ping in measurement, idle and while sending.
- - -> The limit allows two a second.
- - -> 4011 | the sender | It sent more control frames than the control frame limit ([Keepalive](#keepalive)); the partner is closed with 4000
- - -> **The control frame limit.**
- - -> The relay reads at most 60 control frames from one WebSocket between two of its pings (`SFTP_RELAY_MAX_CONTROL_FRAMES`), counted as the WebSocket relay counts them ([WEBSOCKET_RELAY.md](WEBSOCKET_RELAY.md#keepalive)).
- - -> A WebSocket past it is closed with 4002 and its TCP connection ended, and it is dropped if it sends another while the relay waits on its close.
- - -> A browser sends no ping and answers each of the relay's.
- - -> 4002 | `controlFrameLimit` | The browser sent more control frames than the control frame limit.
