- - -> Once the messages queued toward a party count above 1,310,720 bytes (`RELAY_QUEUE_LIMIT_BYTES`, 1.25 MiB), the relay ends the pairing.

- - -> **The per-message fragment limit.**
- - -> A message in more than 64 WebSocket fragments (`RELAY_MAX_MESSAGE_FRAGMENTS`) is refused: `ws` closes its sender with 1008, and the relay closes the partner with 4000 and forwards nothing.
- - -> `ws` holds every fragment of a message until the message completes, outside the bound below, and its default allows 16,384 fragments a message.
