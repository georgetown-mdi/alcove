# The WebSocket relay's socket count, key table wait and ping set, stated as the relay runs them.
- The relay holds at most 48 sockets whose hello has not verified (`RELAY_MAX_UNVERIFIED_SOCKETS`), each counted from its upgrade until its hello verifies or its socket closes. -> The relay holds at most 48 sockets that have not joined a pairing (`RELAY_MAX_UNVERIFIED_SOCKETS`), each counted from its upgrade until it joins a pairing or its socket closes.
- They run synchronously, so a hello that meets a writer holding the table's lock holds up forwarding for every pairing for at most 100 milliseconds (`KEY_TABLE_BUSY_TIMEOUT_MS` in `packages/websocket-relay/src/keyTable.ts`), and is then refused with 1011. -> They run synchronously, so a hello that meets a writer holding the table's lock waits at most 100 milliseconds (`KEY_TABLE_BUSY_TIMEOUT_MS` in `packages/websocket-relay/src/keyTable.ts`) at each of its two reads, and on a newly opened handle also at preparing each read, holding up forwarding for every pairing for up to about 200 milliseconds, or about 400 on a newly opened handle, and is refused with 1011 when a wait runs out.
- The relay pings every socket it holds every 30 seconds (`RELAY_PING_INTERVAL_MS`), a tenth of the 300-second idle timeout of the reference deployment's front, since a browser's `WebSocket` cannot send a ping but answers one. -> The relay pings each socket it has not refused or closed every 30 seconds (`RELAY_PING_INTERVAL_MS`), a tenth of the 300-second idle timeout of the reference deployment's front, since a browser's `WebSocket` cannot send a ping but answers one.
