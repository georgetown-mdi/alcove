# WEBSOCKET_RELAY.md specifies the web application's relay transport, the
# client side of the relay's wire, in a section of its own.
- This document specifies the wire of the WebSocket relay (`packages/websocket-relay`): its endpoint, the frames a party and the relay exchange, how the relay pairs, forwards and closes, its close codes, and its bounds. -> This document specifies the wire of the WebSocket relay (`packages/websocket-relay`): its endpoint, the frames a party and the relay exchange, how the relay pairs, forwards and closes, its close codes, its bounds, and the web application's client.
- - -> The web application's client
- - -> The web application's relay transport (`apps/web/src/psi/transport/websocketRelayConnection.ts`) is one party's side of this wire.
- - -> Its values are working values, as the relay's are.
- - -> **The hello.**
- - -> It opens the relay's `wss` url and, on open, sends its hello and then its start, with no wait between them.
- - -> It refuses a url that has a fragment, which a WebSocket url cannot have, before opening a socket.
- - -> Its proof expires 300 seconds after it is made, the longest lifetime [The WebSocket relay proof](PROTOCOL.md#the-websocket-relay-proof) allows.
- - -> **The waits.**
- - -> It waits 10 minutes for `paired`, the WebRTC rendezvous budget, and then 30 seconds for the partner's start, the channel-open budget ([WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md](WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md#budgets)).
- - -> It refuses a binary message before the partner's start, a start before `paired`, and any text message after the partner's start.
- - -> **The framing.**
- - -> Each binary message is one datagram of the WebRTC framing, written by core's encoder and read through core's bounded reassembler with its caps ([TRANSPORT_BOUNDS.md](TRANSPORT_BOUNDS.md#webrtc-data-channel-inbound-bound)).
- - -> **Pacing.**
- - -> It stops handing a frame's datagrams to the socket while the socket's `bufferedAmount` is at or above 1 MiB, and resumes once it is at or below 256 KiB, the CLI's window ([WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md](WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md#outbound-pacing)).
- - -> A browser `WebSocket` raises no event as its buffer drains, so a waiting send reads `bufferedAmount` every 50 milliseconds.
- - -> **The clean close.**
- - -> It sends the close sentinel behind its last frame and waits up to 5 minutes, the close drain budget, for the partner's close to reach it through the relay or for the partner's own sentinel.
- - -> On reading the partner's sentinel it closes its socket with 1000, the signal the partner's own close waits for.
- - -> Any other end of its socket, an error or a wait that ran out, closes with 4000.
- - -> **Keepalive.**
- - -> It sends no ping, since a browser `WebSocket` cannot; the relay's pings keep its socket open through a parked receive ([Keepalive](#keepalive)).
