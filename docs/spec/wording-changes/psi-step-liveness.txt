- - -> The still-working frame
- - -> On a WebRTC data channel or a WebSocket relay, each party sends still-working frames through its PSI phase, so its partner can wait through a PSI step of any length and stop waiting soon after the party stops.
- - -> The transport rules -- the interval, the partner-silence bound, and what restarts it -- are in [WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md](WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md#the-wait-through-a-partners-psi-step).
- - -> File-sync channels send none (`stillWorkingLayer`, `packages/core/src/exchange/stillWorking.ts`).
- - -> **The frame.**
- - -> The JSON object `{"working":true}`, exactly that: one key, the value `true`.
- - -> It is sent inside the encrypted stream like every frame after the key exchange, so it is authenticated and sequenced like them, and it states nothing but its presence.
- - -> A relay forwarding it sees a small frame of fixed size about every 30 s through the PSI phase, which shows the phase's bounds that the frame sizes already show ([What a relay sees](#linkage-strategies-cascade-and-single-pass)).
- - -> **When it is sent.**
- - -> A party sends one when its PSI phase starts, before anything else in that phase, then whenever the interval passes with nothing sent.
- - -> It sends none after its PSI phase ends or after it sends an abort.
- - -> Its sends never overlap, so the frame keeps the per-direction sequence intact.
- - -> **Where it is accepted.**
- - -> From the start of a party's PSI phase until it reads the partner's first other frame after its own phase ended, a still-working frame is dropped and never reaches a step.
- - -> Outside that window it is a `protocol` error, and so, anywhere, is any other object with a `working` key.
- - -> On a file-sync channel no window exists, so a still-working frame fails the awaited frame's parse as a `protocol` error.
- - -> A party not reading, because it runs its own operation, reads and drops the still-working frames at the head of its inbound queue at each interval; behind a data frame they wait until it reads that frame, charged against the queue's bounds ([TRANSPORT_BOUNDS.md](TRANSPORT_BOUNDS.md#webrtc-data-channel-inbound-bound)).
- - -> **A partner on an earlier build.**
- - -> The first partner frame a party reads in its PSI phase is a still-working frame from a partner on this build.
- - -> A first frame that is data, or the partner's local-fault abort, means the partner sends none: the party refuses it as a `PartnerProtocolRefusalError` naming the partner as on the older version, "Matching could not start, most likely because your partner runs an older version of Alcove.
- - -> Ask them to update Alcove, then run again.", with the abort, where there was one, as its cause.
- - -> Any other abort reaches the phase unchanged.
- - -> A stated limit: a partner on this build whose run faults before its first still-working frame is reported the same way.
- - -> The frame is a wire-format delta within `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 1, as [Wire-format deltas](#wire-format-deltas-existing-frames-only-and-no-version-bump) states for deltas below `1.0.0`.
- - -> **Still-working frames.**
- - -> Through the PSI phase the partner sends a still-working frame at least every 30 s ([WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md, The wait through a partner's PSI step](WEBRTC_TRANSPORT.md#the-wait-through-a-partners-psi-step)), and a party running its own operation does not receive them.
- - -> At each interval of its own the party reads and drops the frames at the head of the queue whose received length is a still-working frame's, and stops at the first frame of another length; a frame of that length that is not one is held and read next, in order.
- - -> Still-working frames queued behind a data frame are not drained: they wait until the consumer reads that frame.
- - -> No bound is added or raised for them: each is charged against the count bound, 1024 messages, and the byte bound above like any frame, and a frame past either fails the connection with the inbound-buffer-overflow `protocol` error.
- - -> Partner silence | 2 min (`PARTNER_SILENCE_BOUND_MS`) | A parked receive with nothing arriving, each part of a frame restarting it, from the partner's first still-working frame to the end of this party's PSI phase. A working value not yet measured in a real browser
- - -> Still-working interval | 30 s (`STILL_WORKING_INTERVAL_MS`) | The longest this party goes without sending through its PSI phase. A working value not yet measured in a real browser
- - -> So that a step of any length is waited through, and a partner that stops is found within minutes, each party sends still-working frames through its PSI phase ([PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md#the-still-working-frame)), and the waiting party ends the run when they stop.
- - -> The web and CLI WebRTC transports and both WebSocket relay clients.
- - -> The file-sync channels send none, and a file-sync run's wait through a partner step is the parked-receive budget alone ([FILE_SYNC.md](FILE_SYNC.md)).
- - -> **Sending.**
- - -> A party sends one when its PSI phase starts, before its PSI engine is built, and then whenever the still-working interval, 30 s, passes with nothing sent.
- - -> Every send through the phase goes through one queue, so a still-working send never overlaps another.
- - -> It stops, once a send in flight settles, when the phase ends, on success, failure or cancellation, or when the party sends an abort.
- - -> **Waiting.**
- - -> The partner's first still-working frame arms the partner-silence bound, 2 min, on a parked receive: a receive with nothing arriving for that long ends the run with the `partner-stopped-responding` cause ([CLI_EVENTS.md](CLI_EVENTS.md)), "Your partner stopped responding during matching: nothing arrived from them for 2 minutes."
- - -> Each frame and each part of a frame being reassembled restarts it, so a large frame on a slow link is not cut off.
- - -> The end of this party's own PSI phase clears it.
- - -> **The whole-frame budget still runs.**
- - -> The parked-receive budget, 1 h or `inactivity_timeout_ms`, bounds each whole frame beside the silence bound, so a relay that trickles bytes cannot hold a run open past it.
- - -> A still-working frame is a frame, so it restarts that budget.
- - -> `inactivity_timeout_ms` does not change the silence bound.
- - -> **Unread frames.**
- - -> A party running its own operation does not receive, so the partner's still-working frames queue.
- - -> On each interval the party reads and drops the still-working frames at the head of the queue, stopping at the first other frame.
- - -> Behind a data frame they wait until the party reads that frame, charged against the inbound queue's message-count and byte bounds like any frame ([TRANSPORT_BOUNDS.md](TRANSPORT_BOUNDS.md#webrtc-data-channel-inbound-bound)).
- - -> **A stated limit: the still-working timer does not observe the PSI engine.**
- - -> A partner whose step hangs while its timer keeps running holds the waiting party with no upper bound.
- - -> Each still-working frame restarts the whole-frame budget, and the silence bound never fires while they arrive.
- - -> **A stated limit: a refusal the drain reads is raised at the next read.**
- - -> Such a refusal is a malformed frame of still-working length, the older-version reading, or a forged, replayed or altered envelope of still-working length.
- - -> The party raises it at its next receive, not when the drain reads it, and carries on with its own step until then.
- - -> A still-working frame is a message to the relay, so the PSI phase never comes near the relay's idle bound ([WEBSOCKET_RELAY.md](WEBSOCKET_RELAY.md#quotas)).
- - -> **Working values.**
- - -> Neither the interval nor the bound is measured in a real browser: a hidden tab's timers, the browser main thread's longest hold, and whether Chrome freezes a tab with an open data channel are open.
- - -> The bound allows the interval, a 12 s CLI hold on each side, and up to 60 s of hidden-tab timer delay.
- - -> **The partner-silence bound**, once armed, on a partner still connected whose frames stop.
- - -> **The parked-receive budget**, with the peer-inactivity refusal (`no message arrived within N ms; the partner appears to have gone silent`), on a partner that sent no still-working frame or once this party's phase has ended.
- - -> On the CLI the refusal, and a send's hand-off refusal under the same budget, add `(the limit inactivity_timeout_ms sets)`.
