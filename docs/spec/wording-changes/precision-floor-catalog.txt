- - -> Terms that take their keys from the built-in rules hold the keys a precision floor selects that the party's own input supplies, so each party narrows its own before the two are compared.
- - -> Where an input supplies none of the selected keys, the terms are left whole, and the exchange refuses before it starts.
- - -> Two files where only one has a phone, email or ZIP column cannot run on the built-in rules: their terms differ, and the exchange refuses at the terms exchange, before any linkage key is sent.
- - -> Such a pair agrees its terms through an invitation instead, and both parties run the terms the invitation holds.
- - -> On either party's refusal, where that party holds both terms documents, core records the names of its own keys its partner does not declare alike (`termsDifferenceLocalKeys`, `packages/core/src/protocolSetup.ts`), and where one of those keys reads a phone, email or ZIP column, the CLI names that column beneath a quick exchange's step.
