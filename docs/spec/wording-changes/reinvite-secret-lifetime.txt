Three write paths stamp it. A successful run's rotation write-back stamps the max-age policy alone, `now + tokenMaxAgeDays`, and clears the bound when no policy is set. A re-invite stamps the earlier of the fresh invitation's own `expires` and that max-age stamp, so with no policy, the default, the fresh setup secret is refused past the invitation's expiry until the first successful run's write-back replaces it, as the command line's key file and the one-shot web inviter refuse it. An operator's in-place edit of `tokenMaxAgeDays` re-derives it under the never-move-later rule (see [Edit-time re-derivation of `expires`](#edit-time-re-derivation-of-expires)).
- Drops `expires` entirely, matching the rotation write-back's `null` clear -- a dropped policy must not leave a stale bound armed. -> Keeps the current `expires`.
- - -> A clear is an unbounded lengthen, so like any lengthen it takes effect at the next rotation's write-back, which with no policy clears the bound.
- - -> A save that leaves the policy as it stands is not an edit of it, and leaves `expires` unchanged.
- - -> The settings card sends `tokenMaxAgeDays` on every save, `null` while the policy is off, so a `null` on a record with no policy, or the stored number, re-derives nothing.
- - -> A re-invite whose invitation expires before the policy's stamp leaves the same offset: the reconstructed anchor lands earlier than the re-invite.
