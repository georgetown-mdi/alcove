- - -> A value matches at most one element on each side, so this is how many candidates the two records share, and summed over a slot, or over a pseudonym, it is how many of that record's candidates matched, which a `psi` cascade's grouping states of each matched record;
- - -> what the multiplicity says about the match.
- - -> Under `edit_distances` an exact match shares the value and every one of its deletions, where a match one edit apart shares few, so the number tells the two apart.
- - -> Under a fan-out it is how many of a record's parts matched;
- - -> from its own records, that a slot whose elements matched `m` times in a round belongs to one of its records with at least `m` real candidates for the key, as its own coverage narrows any slot.
- - -> The two orders within a block, the receiver's and the sender's within each slot, keep from it which of a record's candidates matched, except where the number settles it: a record with one real candidate, or a slot whose matched elements number its record's real candidates, every one of which then matched.
- - -> **A refusal over a party's own rows reaches the partner as an exchange that did not start.**
- - -> Under a candidate set, every refusal building a round can raise comes before the party opens the connection ([Candidate sets under count-only](#candidate-sets-under-count-only)), so no such refusal ends a run the partner has joined, and its round and timing reach the partner as nothing.
- - -> A key with a candidate set adds a finer pattern: a slot's matched elements on the key narrow it to the receiver's records with at least that many real candidates, so a receiver varying its records' candidate counts as well can need fewer keys.
- - -> A key with a candidate set lets such a receiver put up to `w_R` values in one record's block: a count of 1 then says that one of them matched, and its software reads how many of them matched, though not which.
- - -> Under a candidate set the receiver orders each block at random each round ([Candidate sets under count-only](#candidate-sets-under-count-only)), so a position chosen within a block names no particular candidate of the record, such as its exact value.
