- An online `alcove invite` or `alcove accept` writes its configuration and key file only at the terms exchange, once the terms are agreed on, so a refusal leaves it neither, and its step is to agree on the terms and set the exchange up again from a fresh invitation. -> An online `alcove invite` or `alcove accept` writes its key file only at the terms exchange, once the terms are agreed on, and its configuration only once that terms exchange has completed (`runExchange`'s `onTermsExchanged`, `packages/core/src/exchange.ts`), so a refusal leaves it neither, and its step is to agree on the terms and set the exchange up again from a fresh invitation.
- - -> **The caller's step after the terms exchange.**
- - -> `runExchange` awaits the caller's `onTermsExchanged` once on a side whose terms exchange completed, after that side's store and before any linkage key or payload row moves (`packages/core/test/termsExchangedHook.test.ts`).
- - -> A throw from it ends the run after that side sends its partner an abort stating `"a party could not complete its step after the terms exchange"`, so the partner reports that its partner ended the run.
- - -> The online `invite` and `accept` write their configuration there, so a run cut between its store and that step leaves its key file saved and its configuration unwritten, and the CLI states the step for that state ([CLI.md](../CLI.md#online-invitation)).
- - -> It sets it too on the failure of an online `alcove invite` or `alcove accept` that saved its key file and stopped without writing its configuration, whose `message` ends with the step for that state in place of any such remedy (`keyFileSavedConfigNotWrittenRemedy`, `apps/cli/src/failureRemedy.ts`; [CLI.md](../CLI.md#online-invitation)).
