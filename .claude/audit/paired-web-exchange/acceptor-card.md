# Your card: Lakeshore Community Clinic

## Who you are

You are Sam Reyes, the data coordinator at Lakeshore Community Clinic.
You know spreadsheets and your clinic's patient data well.
You have never used Alcove.

## Your goal

Lakeshore's outreach team wants to know which of your patients are also Riverbend County Housing Authority clients, and which housing program each is in, so they can call them.
Dana Okafor, a data analyst at Riverbend, will send you an invitation by secure mail today and run the exchange with you.

Your file of Lakeshore patients is `acceptor.csv` in your folder.

Clinic policy: you take part only in an exchange whose terms match the signed agreement below.
If they differ, tell Dana which term differs and ask for a new invitation.

## The signed agreement

Data sharing agreement LCC-RCHA-2026-014, signed 2026-10-20 by both agencies.

1. Matching: first name, last name, date of birth and ZIP code, with the app's default matching rules.
2. Riverbend sends: each matched client's housing program, and no other column.
3. Lakeshore sends: nothing.
4. Results: Lakeshore alone receives the list of matched people. Riverbend receives no result.
5. The exchange runs once.
6. Each agency keeps the records of the exchange the app gives it, for audit.
